The Reading Room - Privacy Policy

Effective July 2, 2026. Applies to the iOS app and the web app.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google we receive your email address, display name, and (if present) your Google avatar. Using the app creates the content you'd expect a book club to have: clubs and memberships, reading progress, page-tagged reactions, replies, likes and emoji tapbacks, reviews, votes, and any photos you upload as an avatar or club cover.

How it's used

Solely to run the product. Your content is shown to the members of the clubs you belong to, under the app's spoiler rules. We run no ads, no third-party analytics, and no cross-app tracking, and we do not sell or share your data with anyone.

Where it lives

Data is stored with Supabase (Postgres and file storage) protected by row-level security: club content is only readable by that club's members, and spoiler-gated content is filtered by the database itself. Avatar and club-cover images are stored at public but unguessable URLs.

Your choices

You can edit your display name, bio, and photo at any time; delete your own reactions, replies, and reviews; and leave any club. Club creators can delete a club, which permanently removes all of its content for everyone. To delete your account and all associated data, email malcolm.olexa24@gmail.com and it will be removed within 30 days.

Children

The Reading Room is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the effective date above will be updated and the app will surface an announcement.
