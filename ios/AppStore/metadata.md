## Description

The Reading Room is a book club that fits in your pocket - and keeps its
mouth shut about the ending.

Start a club or join one with a 6-character code. Pick your next book
together, then read at your own pace while the app quietly keeps everyone in
sync:

- SPOILER-SAFE REACTIONS. Drop a thought at page 137 and only clubmates who
  have read that far will see it. No warnings to type, no discipline required -
  the gate is built in, enforced server-side, and it just works.
- READING PROGRESS. Log your page and watch the whole club's bookmarks crawl
  toward the finish line. Gentle deadline badges if your club wants them.
- THREADS AND TAPBACKS. Reply to reactions, tap a like or an emoji - every
  conversation stays behind the same spoiler gate as the reaction it hangs on.
- REVIEWS THAT WAIT. Star ratings and full reviews unlock only when you finish
  the book. Finish first, then feast.
- FAIR PICKS. When it's time for the next book, spin the wheel, hold a vote,
  or just pick - the app remembers whose turn it was.
- A FEED THAT FEELS ALIVE. One home feed stitches together every club you're
  in: who finished, who reacted, who's due to pick next.

Sign in with Google and you're reading in under a minute. Made for people who
love finishing a chapter and immediately needing to talk about it - to exactly
the people who've earned it.
